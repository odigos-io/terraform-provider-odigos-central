## 0.1.0

FEATURES:

* **New Provider:** `odigos-central`, configured with `hostname`, `username`, `password` and `insecure_skip_verify` (or the `ODIGOS_HOSTNAME`, `ODIGOS_USERNAME` and `ODIGOS_PASSWORD` environment variables).
* **New Data Source:** `odigos-central_compute_platforms` lists the clusters connected to Odigos Central.
* **New Resource:** `odigos-central_namespace` selects a namespace for instrumentation on a connected cluster.
* **New Resource:** `odigos-central_source` selects a workload for instrumentation on a connected cluster.
